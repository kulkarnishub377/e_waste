Our Mission

At E-Zero, we are committed to creating a sustainable future by responsibly managing electronic waste. Our mission is to ensure that every piece of e-waste is processed in an environmentally safe manner, recovering valuable materials while protecting communities from hazardous substances.

Our Story

Founded in 2009, E-Zero has grown from a small recycling facility in Pune to India's leading e-waste management company. With 25+ processing facilities across the country, we serve 500+ corporate clients and thousands of individual customers.
